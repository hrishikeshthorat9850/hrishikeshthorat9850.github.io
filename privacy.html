Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Rangoli Escape (package com.rangoli.escape) handles information.

Information the game itself stores

The game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos. Your game progress (levels, stars, petals, streaks, badges, colour choices and settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. Android may include this data in your device backup if you have device backup turned on.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To serve, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and app-instance identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostic/crash data.

Ads may be personalised based on this information unless you opt out. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play services

If you choose to rate the game, the in-app rating prompt is provided by Google Play and is covered by Google's privacy policy. Sharing a result opens Android's share sheet; nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

Children

Rangoli Escape is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and Google Play services described above, the game does not share information with third parties.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
